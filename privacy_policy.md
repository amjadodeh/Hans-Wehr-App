## Privacy Policy

**Hans Wehr** (package `com.amjadodeh.hanswehr`, version 4.0 and later) is a free,
open-source Android app that contains the scanned pages of the Hans Wehr dictionary.

**The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

- It requests no permissions and does not use the internet.
- All dictionary pages are bundled inside the app, so nothing is downloaded.
- Your recent searches are stored only on your device, to offer suggestions in the
  search box. You can delete them at any time from Settings → Clear search history.
- There are no advertisements, analytics, crash reporting, or in-app purchases.

The "Help and Feedback" and "Source code" items open the project's GitHub page in
your web browser. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to anything you do there.

Earlier versions of the app, published by the original developer on Google Play under
the package `com.etjaal.arabicalmanac`, used Google AdMob and Firebase and were covered
by a different policy. This policy applies only to the rebuilt app distributed from
<https://github.com/amjadodeh/Hans-Wehr-App>.

**Changes to this policy** will be posted in this file in the repository.

**Contact:** open an issue at <https://github.com/amjadodeh/Hans-Wehr-App/issues>.
